Why linking words matter

Assignments are written in formal language. Linking (transition) words join ideas, sentences and paragraphs, and show the direction, order and flow of your argument. They make your work stronger and easier to follow.

Tip: when a transition starts a sentence, follow it with a comma. Between two main ideas, use a semi-colon or full stop.

Linking inside a sentence

Words that link ideas within one sentence are called coordinating conjunctions. Don't worry about the term. Think about the function.
